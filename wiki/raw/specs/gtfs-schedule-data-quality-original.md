Owner: Evan Siroky
Responsibilities: Dashboards, SOPs, ensuring data stewards maintain quality, automation for SOPs.

Contract: GTFS Schedule Data Quality is critical for complete, accurate, and precise information about public transit operations in California. Baseline schedule from which GTFS Realtime data refers to.

Consumers: Reports website, GTFS Digest. Depends on: California Transit Database.

SLA: Weekly checks of download errors, validation errors, expiring/about-to-expire warnings. Annual holiday service accuracy checks (Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's Day).

Lifecycle: So long as detailed data about transit operations is desired, GTFS Schedule Data Quality checking will occur.
